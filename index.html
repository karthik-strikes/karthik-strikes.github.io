About me

I research human-in-the-loop AI for evidence synthesis in medicine at the University of Pennsylvania, working with the Center for Integrative Global Oral Health and Penn CIS. I build systems that turn published clinical trials into living guidelines, and study speech and language markers of health in older adults.

My work sits where the protocol of evidence-based medicine meets what language models can actually be trusted to do. Systematic reviews demand independent dual extraction, adjudication, and a traceable record of how every value was produced, and none of that survives handing a PDF to a model and accepting the output. EviStreams, the platform I lead, keeps domain experts in control at each of those stages.

Research interestsHuman-in-the-loop AI for systematic reviews · NLP for health · multimodal speech & language analysis · agentic LLM systems for clinical evidence
